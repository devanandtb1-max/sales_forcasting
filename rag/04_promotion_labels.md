## What do these labels establish?
The labels establish how the export classifies records. CM, L1, and ST share the generic name Added by gmGmcUpd; that name does not explain their business meaning or prove the codes are equivalent. The counts describe records, not unique campaigns or relative sales impact.

MB is labelled MULTI BUYS. The label does not provide a required purchase quantity, a customer offer formula, or supplier MOQ. Supplier carton requirements cannot be inferred from a consumer promotion label. Similarly, labels containing a duration are not substitutes for the actual recorded dates.
